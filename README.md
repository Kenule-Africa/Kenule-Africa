# Kenule Africa

Kenule Africa is a technology company building products, platforms and digital talent for Africa's future. It is a registered Nigerian company (RC: 8380017) based in Jos, Plateau State, and its work is guided by one idea: technology should be designed for how Africa actually works, not copied from elsewhere.

This repository is the official Kenule Africa website, live at [kenuleafrica.xyz](https://kenuleafrica.xyz).

## What Kenule Africa does

Kenule Africa brings fintech infrastructure, free digital skills education, regional ecosystem building and creative experiences together into one connected ecosystem.

| Initiative | What it is |
|---|---|
| **DeyPay** | Stablecoin-powered banking rails built for how Africa moves money: multi-currency wallets, borderless transfers and savings. |
| **LegitLab Academy** | A tuition-free, cohort-based talent accelerator with five practical tracks: UI/UX Design, Web Development, Digital Marketing, YouTube and Video Creation, and Web3. |
| **Jos Tech Expo** | A Northern Nigerian technology and innovation summit bringing founders, builders, policy leaders and investors together in Plateau State. |
| **TOMORROW** | An exclusive gathering in Port Harcourt that brings the people shaping tomorrow into one room today. |
| **ShoutOut** | A creative agency for creators and brands: digital marketing, influencer management, talent development and brand partnerships. |
| **Builders Circle** | Kenule Africa's community for software engineers, designers and tech enthusiasts to learn, share and build together. |
